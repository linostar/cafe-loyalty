How it works

Three steps, and nothing for your customers to install.

  1. 1

    Customers scan your QR

    A printed code at your counter. They enter their name and mobile number and get their card in seconds.

  2. 2

    Baristas add stamps

    The counter app runs on any phone. Scan the card or type the number; it keeps working when the internet drops.

  3. 3

    You see what works

    Busy and quiet hours, regulars who stopped coming, and what each offer brought in, on your dashboard.

Made for how cafés run

  • In their wallet

    Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or a web card in the browser, in your colours, with your logo and your stamp shape.

  • Quiet-hour offers

    Fill slow afternoons with offers on the cards of customers who opted in, never priced below the margin you set.

  • Bring regulars back

    When a regular stops coming, their card can get a welcome-back offer on its own.

  • Stamps you can trust

    Each barista signs in with a PIN, repeat stamps are held back, and unusual visits wait for your review.

  • Feedback and reviews

    After a visit, customers can send you a private message or leave a review on Google.

  • Private by design

    Your café sees a customer's card and name, not their number. Customers can delete their card at any time.
